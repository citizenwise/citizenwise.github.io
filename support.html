Email support@citizenwise.app. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Restore my Full access: open the Full access screen and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

A question or answer looks wrong: email us with the question number. Every question links to its official source so you can check it.

Delete my data: your progress is on your phone and, if backup is on, in your own iCloud or Google backup. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the phone. To delete the backup too, see “When you uninstall the app” in the privacy policy (on iPhone, a small free-tier counter for the current week can also remain). For purchase records, email us. We don't hold an account for you.

Microphone (US app): used only for interview practice, recognized on your device, never recorded. You can always type instead.
