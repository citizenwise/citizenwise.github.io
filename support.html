Support

Email support@citizenwise.app. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Restore my Country Pass: open the Country Pass screen and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

A question or answer looks wrong: email us with the question number. Every question links to its official source so you can check it.

Delete my data: your progress is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app deletes it (on iPhone, a small free-tier counter for the current week can remain; see the privacy policy). For purchase records, email us. We don't hold an account for you.

Microphone (US app): used only for interview practice, recognised on your device, never recorded. You can always type instead.
