About Citizenwise

Citizenwise is a study app for citizenship tests. This page says who we are and how we work.

Who makes Citizenwise

Citizenwise is made by Mayank Kunwwar, an independent developer in Nainital, India. You can write to us at support@citizenwise.app. A person reads every email.

We are not the government

Citizenwise is not a government website and not a government agency. We are not connected to USCIS in the United States or the BAMF in Germany. We are not lawyers, and we don't give legal advice. For your own case, ask the official office, an advice centre or a lawyer.

Where our facts come from

Every test question links to its official source: the USCIS study materials in the United States and the BAMF question catalogue in Germany. Each country page says when we last checked its facts. Found a mistake? Please write to us, and we will check it and fix it.

How we make money

The questions and practice tests are free. Some extras are a one-time purchase in the app, never a subscription. There are no ads, and we don't sell your data.

How to avoid scams

  • No government agency asks you to pay an app to “book” your test or interview.
  • Pay official fees only on the official government website.
  • Never send your ID documents or passport to an app.
  • In the United States, USCIS explains common scams: uscis.gov: avoid scams.
  • In Germany, only your local citizenship office (Einbürgerungsbehörde) decides on your application.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site: with a screen reader, with large text, with the keyboard only, and in dark mode. If something does not work for you, please tell us at support@citizenwise.app.

Citizenwise is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to USCIS, BAMF or any government. It is not legal advice and not a substitute for the advice of an attorney.
