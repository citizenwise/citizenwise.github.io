Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Short version: we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and your study progress stays on your devices (and in your own Google backup or iCloud, if that is on). We never see it.

Who we are

Citizenwise is operated by Mayank Singh Kunwar (Citizenwise), 111, Vasudevpuram, RTO Road, Haldwani, Uttarakhand 263139, India ("we"; the developer goes by Mayank Kunwwar). See also the legal notice. We are the controller for the personal data described here. Contact: support@citizenwise.app.

What stays on your device

  • Study progress (answers, scores, bookmarks, review schedule, chosen state or federal state, your test date and checklist ticks) is stored on your device and, on Android, in your own Google backup if backup is on, and on iPhone and iPad in your own iCloud if you are signed in (see "Android backup" and "iCloud" below). We never receive it. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the phone (see "When you uninstall the app" below).
  • Free-tier record (on your device): to keep the weekly free allowance fair and the welcome-price deadline real, the app keeps a small record for each country: how many free practice tests and practice interviews you started in each of the last 8 weeks, the latest time the app was used, when you first opened it, when purchases first became available in the app and when your welcome price started. It contains no name, account or device ID, and we never receive it. On iPhone and iPad it is kept in the iOS Keychain; iOS may keep it after you delete the app, and it is removed when you erase the device. On Android it is kept in Google Play services Block Store, so reinstalling the app does not reset it; with Android backup on, it is also kept in your own Google backup (end-to-end encrypted where your phone supports it). It is removed when you reset the phone and delete its Google backup.
  • Android backup: on Android, your study progress, settings (including the random purchase ID RevenueCat creates, so a restored app matches your purchase again), weekly free-test counters and the error log (see below) may be included in your own Google backup (Android Auto Backup) and restored when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. We never see this backup. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup.
  • iCloud (iPhone and iPad): when you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your study progress (the same items as above, without purchases) in your own iCloud key-value storage, so it comes back after you reinstall the app and is shared between your iPhone and iPad. Apple keeps it under its own rules; we never receive it. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud, or delete it by deleting the app's iCloud data there.
  • Transfer to another phone: Settings → Backup & sync can save your study progress as a file that you send yourself (AirDrop, email, a cloud drive) and open in the app on another phone. You choose where it goes; nothing goes through us. The file contains your progress, bookmarks, chosen state and test date, but no purchases.
  • ZIP code: the congressional-district lookup runs on your phone from bundled Census data. The ZIP code you type is not stored or sent.
  • Content updates: at most once a day, the app downloads a small content file with a security check (so the app knows it really comes from us) from citizenwise.app (US app: official names and dates, for example your senators). It sends no personal data: every app makes the same request. Like any web request, the host (GitHub Pages) sees your IP address and may keep logs under its own policy. If you stay offline, nothing is downloaded and the app uses the answers built into it. The Germany app does not download content.
  • Error log: if something goes wrong in the app, it keeps a short technical note of the last 20 errors on your phone, and with Android backup on also in your own Google backup with the rest of the app's data (the kind of error, where in the app it happened, the app version and the time). Next to it, a separate usage log keeps the last 10 lines about the free weekly practice limit (for example "practice test used, now 3"), so that a limit question can be checked; it never pushes errors out of the error log. It contains no name, answers, ZIP code or other personal details and is never sent automatically. If you tap "Send report", your email app opens a message to support@citizenwise.app with the log as text; you can read it first, and nothing is sent unless you send that email.
  • Voice (US app, interview practice): if you allow the microphone, speech is recognized on your device by your phone's speech recognition. We do not record, store or receive your audio.
  • Reading voice (US app): questions are read aloud by your phone's own built-in text-to-speech voice. The app always asks for US English and picks a US English voice that is installed on your phone; it never picks a voice that needs the internet. On most phones the voice works offline. If your phone has no offline US English voice, its speech engine may use the internet to read the app's text (the questions and answers of the app, never anything you typed or said); the app's Settings then shows how to install one. The app keeps only the voice you picked and the speed, in its settings on your device.
  • Study reminders: scheduled on your phone as local notifications. No push service is used.

When you uninstall the app

Uninstalling deletes everything the app keeps in its own storage on the phone: study progress, settings, the error log, downloaded translation language packs and scheduled reminders. It does not delete:

  • a copy in your own Google backup (Android, if backup is on). Google keeps it under its own rules and restores it if you reinstall; you can delete it in your Google account (Google Drive → Backups) or turn backup off in Settings → Google → Backup.
  • the free-tier record described above (Block Store on Android, the iOS Keychain on iPhone and iPad). It is removed when you reset or erase the device (and, on Android, delete its Google backup).
  • your purchases, which stay with Apple or Google, and the RevenueCat purchase record (we delete it on request, see "Your rights").
  • emails you sent us (see "How long we keep data").

What we and our service providers receive

  • Purchases: Apple or Google handle payment as the seller and are responsible for their own processing (see their privacy policies). To check and restore your Full access purchase (and AI Plus, where offered) the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as our processor. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID it creates, your purchase receipt and transaction history, the app version, and technical connection data such as your IP address, country and currency. It does not receive your name, email or card details. RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • Free interview check (US app on Android only): Android's "Clear storage" deletes the free-tier record above, so before a free practice interview the app asks our small server whether this phone has already used this week's free one. It sends a one-way code made from the phone's Android ID for this app (a SHA-512 hash; the ID itself is never sent and cannot be worked out from the code), the country ("us"), the kind of use and your time-zone offset. The server stores only that code, the time of the free interview and when it refills (next Monday), and deletes the entry within a day after it refills. Nothing is stored for Full access users or on iPhone. The server runs on Cloudflare Workers (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) as our processor, which handles technical connection data such as your IP address to deliver the request; we keep no request logs. Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Support emails: if you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. Email is handled by our domain's mail forwarding service and Google (Gmail).
  • Contact form: if you use the contact form, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives what you type (your name if you give it, your email address and your message) plus technical connection data such as your IP address, and forwards it to our support inbox so we can reply. Its servers may be outside the EU, including in the USA. We keep the message only in our support inbox, like an email (see "How long we keep data"). The form is optional: you can always email us instead.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security and delivery. We use no cookies, analytics or trackers on this site. GitHub privacy statement.
  • Official source links open in your browser; the visited site's own privacy policy applies.

Translations

If you use the optional translate button, the app translates on your phone. On Android this uses Google ML Kit: the app downloads a language pack (about 30 MB) from Google once, after you agree, and when you use the feature Google receives technical data to deliver the pack and to measure the feature (device model, operating system, app name and version, the chosen languages, performance and error data, and a random ML Kit installation ID). The text you translate stays on your phone. On iPhone, Apple's on-device Translation feature is used, under Apple's privacy policy. Google ML Kit data disclosure.

AI explanations

AI explanations are not available yet. Before they are, this policy will name the AI provider. When you ask for one, the question, the official answer and your chosen answer are sent to that provider; if you type a follow-up question, your text is sent too, so please don't include personal details. The provider may not use it to train its models. AI answers are always labelled as AI.

What we don't do

No accounts or sign-up. No advertising or ad tracking, no advertising ID, no selling or sharing of personal data (including as defined by US state privacy laws). No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own; the diagnostics Google ML Kit collects for the optional translation on Android are described under "Translations". If that changes, we update this policy first.

Do Not Track

Do Not Track: we don't track visitors across sites or apps, so we treat every visitor the same whether or not the browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Legal bases (EU/UK users)

The first release is offered in the United States only; distribution in the EU starts with the German app. This section applies to EU/UK users of the apps and this website.

  • Purchase checks and restores: performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Answering your emails and contact-form messages: Art. 6(1)(b), or our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Website security logs: our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Translation diagnostics (Google ML Kit, Android): our legitimate interest in a working translation feature you chose to use (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Storage on your device: only what is strictly necessary to provide the app features you use.

How long we keep data

Support emails and contact-form messages: up to 24 months after the conversation ends, then deleted. Free interview check entries: until the free interview refills (the next Monday) plus up to one day. RevenueCat purchase records: as long as you may restore a purchase, plus any period tax law requires; we delete them on request where the law allows.

International transfers

We are based in India; RevenueCat, GitHub and Google are in the USA. Transfers from the EU/UK rely on the providers' data-protection terms, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. A contact-form message passes through FormSubmit, possibly in the USA, because you ask us to receive it (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)); if you prefer, email us directly.

EU representative

We have not appointed a representative in the EU (GDPR Art. 27): our processing is only occasional, not large-scale and involves no special categories of data and no server-side profiling. Your study data stays on your device, purchases go through Apple or Google, and we receive only the support messages you send us.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Email support@citizenwise.app; we reply within one month. To delete purchase records, send us the date of purchase and the store; we delete the RevenueCat customer record (your purchase stays with Apple or Google). In the EU/UK you can also complain to a data-protection authority, for example the one where you live.

Children

The apps are made for adults preparing for a citizenship test. They are not directed at children under 13 (US) or under 16 (EU), and we do not knowingly collect children's data. If you think a child has sent us personal data, email us and we will delete it.
