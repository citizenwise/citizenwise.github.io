Citizenwise

Study for your citizenship test with the real official question lists, practice tests that follow the official rules, and (in the US app) spoken interview practice.

  • US Civics Test: Citizenwise: the 2025 civics test (128 questions), practice interview, reading and writing practice, and N-400 interview English (the words and questions on the form, read aloud; not legal advice).
  • Einbürgerungstest: Citizenwise: all 460 questions of the "Leben in Deutschland" test, including your federal state.

Every question links to its official source. No ads. Every question stays free. In the US app, practice tests are free and unlimited, with 1 spoken practice interview a week; one-time Full access (not a subscription) adds unlimited spoken practice interviews, spoken N-400 practice for every section, the “Why” and Listen for every topic, and translations. In the German app, Full access adds unlimited practice tests.
