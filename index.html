Citizenwise

Study for your citizenship test with the real official question lists, practice tests that follow the official rules, and (in the US app) spoken interview practice.

  • US Civics Test: Citizenwise: the 2025 civics test (128 questions), practice interview, and reading and writing practice.
  • Einbürgerungstest: Citizenwise: all 460 questions of the "Leben in Deutschland" test, including your federal state.

Every question links to its official source. No ads. Every question stays free. In the US app, practice tests are free and unlimited, with 1 spoken practice interview a week. Full access, a one-time purchase (not a subscription), is coming soon. It will add unlimited spoken practice interviews, the “Why” and Listen for every topic, and translations. In the German app, Full access will add unlimited practice tests.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.
