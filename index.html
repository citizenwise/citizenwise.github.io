Citizenwise

Study for your citizenship test with the real question catalogues, mock exams that follow the official rules, and (in the US app) spoken interview practice.

  • US Civics Test: Citizenwise: the 2025 civics test (128 questions), mock interview, reading and writing practice.
  • Einbürgerungstest: Citizenwise: all 460 questions of the "Leben in Deutschland" test, including your federal state.

Every question links to its official source. No ads. Core practice is free, and a one-time Country Pass (not a subscription) unlocks unlimited mock exams and interviews.
