US Civics Test: Citizenwise

The US naturalization civics test: the 2025 test (128 questions) or the 2008 test (100 questions), picked from the date you filed your application. Spoken practice interview, reading and writing practice, and your state's senators, representative and governor.

Einbürgerungstest: Citizenwise

The German "Leben in Deutschland" test: the questions of the current list, including the questions for your federal state, and practice tests with the real rules (33 questions, 60 minutes, 17 to pass).

Honest by design
