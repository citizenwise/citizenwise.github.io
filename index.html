The path to citizenship, in 4 steps

  1. 1

    Check if you can apply

    Each country has rules, for example how long you have lived there. Your country's official website explains them.

  2. 2

    Prepare for the test

    Most countries have a test about the country and a language test. Citizenwise helps with the test about the country.

  3. 3

    The interview or appointment

    You answer questions and show your documents.

  4. 4

    Become a citizen

    Many countries end with a ceremony.

We help with step 2. For your own case, ask an official advice centre or a lawyer. We don't give legal advice.
