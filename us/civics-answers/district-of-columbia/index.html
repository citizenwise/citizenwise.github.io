District of Columbia civics test answers: senators, delegate

District of Columbia is not a state, so some civics test answers are different there.

Checked on October 20, 2026 · next check November 4, 2026

Who is one of your state's US senators?

Answer that D.C. has no U.S. senators. USCIS accepts this answer from residents of D.C. and the US territories.

Name your US representative

Your delegate counts: Eleanor Holmes Norton (D).

Governor and capital

D.C. has no governor and, as it is not a state, no state capital. USCIS accepts these answers from D.C. residents.

The same everywhere

  • President: Donald J. Trump
  • Vice President: JD Vance
  • Speaker of the House: Mike Johnson
  • Chief Justice: John Roberts
