Puerto Rico civics test answers: senators, delegate

Puerto Rico is not a state, so some civics test answers are different there. Checked on 2026-10-20.

Who is one of your state's US senators?

Answer that Puerto Rico has no U.S. senators. USCIS accepts this answer from residents of D.C. and the US territories.

Name your US representative

Your resident commissioner counts: Pablo José Hernández (D).

Governor and capital

Name the governor and the capital of Puerto Rico; see usa.gov for the current governor.

  • President: Donald J. Trump
  • Vice President: JD Vance
  • Speaker of the House: Mike Johnson
