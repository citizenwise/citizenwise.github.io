All 128 US Civics Test Questions and Answers (2025 test)

If you filed your citizenship application (Form N-400) on or after October 20, 2025, you take the 2025 civics test: the officer asks up to 20 of these 128 questions, out loud, and you pass with 12 correct answers. Not sure which test is yours? See the 2008 test (100 questions).

Questions marked * are the 20 questions for applicants 65 or older with 20 or more years as a permanent resident (the 65/20 rule).

Practice them out loud: the free Citizenwise app asks these questions the way the officer does, checks your spoken answer and follows the real rules. Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

Questions about the civics test

Which civics test do I take, the 2025 or the 2008 test?

It depends on when you filed your citizenship application (Form N-400). Filed on or after October 20, 2025: the 2025 test (128 questions). Filed before: the 2008 test (100 questions).

How many questions do I need to answer correctly?

2025 test: the officer asks up to 20 questions and you pass with 12 correct answers; the officer stops at 12 correct or 9 wrong. 2008 test: the officer asks 10 questions and you pass with 6 correct answers.

What is the 65/20 rule?

If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years or more when you file, you study only the 20 questions marked with * and may take the civics test in the language of your choice. The officer asks 10 of them and you pass with 6 correct.

Is the civics test multiple choice?

No. The officer asks the questions out loud and you answer out loud. The Citizenwise app practices it the same way.
