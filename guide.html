Start in 3 steps

  1. Open the app. Enter your ZIP code, or choose your state.
  2. Answer the first few questions. Then tell the app when your interview is.
  3. Practice a few minutes every day. Tap the big button on Home. It always shows your next step.

First start

You do not need an account. The app asks you a few things, one step at a time.

  1. A short note. Citizenwise is a study app. It is not USCIS. Tap "Start".
  2. Your ZIP code. Some answers depend on where you live, for example your senators and your Representative. Type your ZIP code. The app finds your state and your Representative. It works offline. Your ZIP code stays on your phone.
    • No ZIP code at hand? Tap "Don’t know your ZIP code?", then "Choose your state instead".
    • Not now? Tap "Skip for now". You can choose it later in Settings.
  3. The microphone. In the practice interview you answer out loud. Tap "Allow microphone". If you prefer to type, tap "I’ll type instead".
  4. A few first questions. This shows you where to start. A low score is fine.
  5. "When is your interview?" Choose "In 1–2 months", "Pick a date" or "Not sure yet". With a date, tap "Start my plan". Without one, tap "Start practicing".

Which test do you take?

There are two civics tests. The date you sent your citizenship application (Form N-400) decides which one you take:

  • Sent before Oct 20, 2025: the 2008 test (100 questions).
  • Sent on or after Oct 20, 2025, or not sent yet: the 2025 test (128 questions).

The app starts with the 2025 test. If you sent your application before Oct 20, 2025, change it: go to Settings and tap "Your test: 2025 (128 questions) · Change". Enter the "Received date" from the letter USCIS sent you. Tap "Save date".

Home

Home shows how you are doing and what to do next.

  • Top: a greeting and the days until your interview, for example "Interview in 30 days". The gear icon at the top right opens Settings.
  • "Ready score": the ring shows a percent. Half of it comes from the questions you know. Half comes from your last 5 practice tests. It is only a guide. It can’t tell you if you will pass. Tap "Ready score" to read more.
  • The big button: your next step. It changes. For example: "Continue today’s plan", "Review 12 questions", "Start a practice interview" or "Continue: (a topic)".
  • "Today’s goal": how many questions you did today, and a dot for each day of the week you studied.
  • Below: quick links, for example "Practice all questions" and "Interview practice". About two weeks before your interview, the "Interview-day kit" appears. It says what to bring.

Practice

Tap the "Practice" tab at the bottom. At the top you find:

  • "Practice all questions": go through every question.
  • "Review": the questions you should practice again today. The number shows how many. "All caught up ✓" means nothing is due.
  • "Bookmarks": questions you saved.
  • "Listen (hands-free)": the phone reads questions and answers aloud.

Topics

Below is the list of topics. Each topic shows how far you are, for example "5 of 12 practiced". After 5 answers in a topic, you also see how many you got right, for example "· 80% correct". If a topic is hard for you, it says "Needs work". Tap a topic to practice it.

How a question works

  1. Read the question. Tap "Listen" to hear it.
  2. Think of your answer. Then tap "Show answer".
  3. Be honest: tap "I knew it" or "Not yet". You can also swipe: right if you knew it, left if not yet.

Questions you did not know come back in "Review". In Review you can tap "I know this well" to take a question out.

  • Bookmark: tap 🔖 on a question to save it. Find it later in "Bookmarks".
  • "Why": after you answer, a short box explains the answer in plain words, with its official source. It is there for the 2025 test.
  • Words with ⓘ: tap them to see what they mean.

Listen hands-free

Open "Listen (hands-free)" on the Practice tab. The phone reads a question, waits so you can think, then reads the answer. It works offline. It is good for a walk or a bus ride. Choose a topic, "To review" or "All questions". You can also choose the speed. Free: the first topic. Every topic comes with Full access.

Practice tests

A practice test works like the civics part of the real interview. Start it from the "Interview" tab: tap "Start practice test".

  • 2025 test: up to 20 questions. You pass with 12 correct.
  • 2008 test: up to 10 questions. You pass with 6 correct.
  • The test stops early, like the real one: when you have passed, or when you can no longer pass.

The phone reads each question aloud. You can answer in three ways: "Use the mic", "Type instead" or "I’ll say it myself" (you then check your answer with the official answers). Tap "Repeat" to hear the question again, or "Slower" to hear it slowly.

At the end you see your result. To practice your mistakes, tap "Review these 3" (the number changes) or "Review this one". Practice tests are free, with no limit.

If you close the app during a test, you can go on later: tap "Continue your test". If you tap "Leave", your answers so far do not count.

Practice interview

Tap the "Interview" tab (the microphone icon). Here you practice the whole interview, out loud:

  1. First the oath: a promise to tell the truth. Then some small talk. This part does not count.
  2. The civics questions, with the same rules as the practice test.
  3. The reading test: read 1 of 3 sentences correctly to pass.
  4. The writing test: listen and type 1 of 3 sentences correctly to pass.

Tap "Start interview" to begin. Tap the microphone and answer out loud. If the app heard you wrong, you can try again. You can also type, or say the answer and check it yourself.

Your privacy: your phone turns your voice into text. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

65 or older? If you were 65 or older and a permanent resident for 20 years or more when you filed, turn on "I was 65+ and a permanent resident for 20+ years when I filed". Then you study only 20 questions. The app cannot decide this for you.

This is only a practice score. At the real interview, the USCIS officer decides.

Free: 1 practice interview a week. It counts only once you answer the first civics question. You get a new one every Monday. Practice and practice tests stay free.

Progress

Tap the "Progress" tab. You see:

  • "Last 7 days": how many questions you did each day.
  • "Correct answers, last 4 weeks": how your answers get better week by week.
  • Badges: small rewards for your work. A locked badge tells you how to earn it.
  • "Recent practice tests": each test with ✓ or ✗, your score and the date.

Study plan and reminders

Do you know the date of your interview? Then let the app plan for you.

  1. On Home, tap "Set your test date". (Or go to Settings and tap "Study reminder".)
  2. Enter the date. Tap "Save date".
  3. The app spreads all questions over the days before your interview. Each day you see "Today’s goal".
  4. Tap "Remind me daily at 18:00" to get a daily reminder. Your phone may ask you to allow notifications.

The date stays on your phone. To change it, tap "Remove date" and enter a new one.

Translations

The real test is in English. But a translation can help you understand a question.

  1. Go to Settings. Tap "Translate into". Choose your language from the list. You can search for it.
  2. In practice, tap the translate button on the question card. It shows the word "Translate" in your language.
  • A computer on your phone translates. It may make mistakes. No person has checked it.
  • The first time, your phone may need to download the language. On Android this uses Wi-Fi, unless you turn on "Download translations with mobile data (not only Wi-Fi)".
  • Translations do not show in practice tests.
  • Translations are part of Full access (see Full access). Without it, the card shows "Translate · Full access".

Some people may take the civics test in their own language, because of their age and years as a permanent resident. If that is you, turn on "I may take the civics test in my own language". Tap "Who can use this?" to read the rules. Then translations also work in practice tests. The app cannot decide this for you, and it is not legal advice.

Settings

Tap the gear icon at the top right of Home. In Settings you can change:

  • "Full access": on Android, a card at the top shows what Full access includes.
  • Your state: "Your state" or "State: … (change)".
  • Your test: "Your test: 2025 (128 questions) · Change".
  • "Study reminder": your test date and daily reminder.
  • "Text size": from 90% to 160%. A sample sentence shows the size.
  • "Reading voice": choose a "Voice" and tap "Play sample" to hear it. Choose the "Speed". "0.9× (normal)" is the normal speed; "0.75×" is slower. This is your phone’s computer voice, not a real officer.
  • "Translate into": your language for translations.
  • "Backup & sync": free. If your phone’s backup is on, your progress goes to your own iCloud or Google account. We never see it.
  • "Transfer to another phone": tap "Send my progress" to save your progress as a file. On the new phone, tap "Open a progress file". This works between iPhone and Android.
  • "Appearance": "Match phone", "Light" or "Dark". You can also pick "Your color".
  • "Vibration": on or off.
  • "Send feedback": write to us.

Full access: what is free

Most of the app is free, with no ads and no account.

Always free

  • Every civics question, what counts as correct, and its official source
  • Unlimited practice tests
  • "Review" and "Bookmarks"
  • 1 spoken practice interview a week
  • "Why" and Listen for the first topic
  • The backup of your progress

Full access

Full access is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. Nothing renews. It adds:

  • Unlimited practice interviews (you speak)
  • "Why" and Listen for every topic
  • Translations

On Android it costs USD 9.99 on Google Play. The app shows the price before you buy. To buy, go to Settings. At the top, on the "Full access" card, tap "See what’s included".

On iPhone, Full access is coming soon. Until then, "Why", Listen and translations are open for every topic. The practice interview stays at 1 a week. Everything else in the app stays free.

New phone? Full access usually comes back by itself when you are online. If not: sign in with the same Google account you used to buy. In Settings, tap "See what’s included". Under "Already paid on another phone?", tap "Restore purchases". You do not pay again.

Tips for older learners

  • Bigger text: Settings, "Text size". Try 130% or 160%.
  • A slower voice: Settings, "Reading voice", "Speed": choose "0.75×". In a question, tap "Slower" to hear it slowly again.
  • Typing instead of speaking: in the interview or a practice test, tap "Type instead". Or say the answer to yourself and tap "I’ll say it myself".
  • Hard words: tap words with ⓘ.
  • Short and often: 5 minutes a day is better than one long day. The daily reminder helps.
  • Listen: use "Listen (hands-free)" and just listen, like the radio.
  • Ask for help: a family member or friend can set up the app with you the first time.
